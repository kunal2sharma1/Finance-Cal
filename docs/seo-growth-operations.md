# FinCalc SEO Growth Operations

## Immediate setup

1. Verify `https://finance-cal.kunal2sharma1.workers.dev/` in Google Search Console.
2. Submit `https://finance-cal.kunal2sharma1.workers.dev/sitemap.xml`.
3. Verify the same property in Bing Webmaster Tools and submit the sitemap.
4. Record the initial baseline: indexed URLs, impressions, clicks, average position, top queries, top landing pages, countries and devices.

## Weekly workflow

- Review Search Console Performance for the last 7 days versus the previous 7 days.
- Identify pages with high impressions and low CTR.
- Identify queries ranking around positions 5–20.
- Identify calculators receiving impressions but little traffic.
- Check indexing reports for excluded or error URLs.
- Review sitemap status and discovered/indexed URL counts.
- Compare country performance to decide where localized content deserves expansion.
- Use the highest-confidence opportunities to create or improve one calculator page, one guide cluster, or one internal-linking path.

## Priority rules

1. Fix indexing or canonical problems before creating more content.
2. Improve pages already receiving impressions before chasing entirely new keywords.
3. Prioritize queries with clear calculator intent.
4. Build supporting guides around calculators that already show search demand.
5. Do not create thin country pages or duplicate calculator pages only to increase URL count.

## What not to claim

Traffic, rankings, impressions, clicks, indexing and conversion metrics must come from connected analytics/Search Console data. Do not estimate or fabricate them.
